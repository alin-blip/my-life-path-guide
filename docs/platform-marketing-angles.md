## THEME 1 — Burnout Recovery

### 1.1 Burnout Test (RO + EN)
- **Hook**: *"Find out in 20 questions how close you are to founder burnout."*
- **Pain**: Founders normalize exhaustion until the body forces a stop.
- **Promise**: Objective radar chart across 6 dimensions + a personalized 3-email recovery plan.
- **Mechanism**: 20 validated questions → weighted scoring per dimension → radar visualization + benchmark.
- **CTA**: `/burnout-test` (RO) or `/burnout-test-en` (EN)
- **Use cases**:
  - 📧 "You might already be in Stage 3 burnout — here's the 2-min test"
  - 🎥 Webinar: "The 6 hidden signs of founder burnout no coach talks about"
  - 📖 Chapter: "The Burnout Ladder — from Passion to Collapse"
  - 📱 IG carousel: 6 dimensions + one-line each

### 1.2 Burnout Story (10-day nurture sequence — NEW)
- **Hook**: *"A 10-day story that changes how you think about work."*
- **Pain**: Every founder thinks "I'm different, I can push harder." Then they can't.
- **Promise**: 10 short stories, one per day, that reframe hustle culture.
- **Mechanism**: Automatic daily send, language-aware (RO/EN), personalized greeting, stop on ebook/challenge purchase.
- **CTA (all days)**: Try CEO Mind OS free for 5 days → `/challenge-7-zile`
- **Use cases**:
  - 📧 Each of 10 subject lines becomes its own campaign angle
  - 🎥 Live: "Reading Day 3 out loud + Q&A"
  - 📱 Cross-post one story per day as newsletter/thread

### 1.3 Burnout Alerts (in-product)
- **Hook**: *"Your dashboard warns you before your body does."*
- **Pain**: Burnout is invisible until it isn't.
- **Promise**: Automatic detection from your daily patterns.
- **CTA**: Sign up → track for 7 days
- **Use cases**: 📧 "The app that flagged burnout before I noticed"
